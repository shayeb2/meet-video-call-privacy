Privacy Policy

Meet Video Call
Last updated: October 5, 2026

Meet Video Call ("we", "our", or "the app") is a random video communication application that allows users to meet and communicate with other users through live video calls.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Profile Information

When you create a profile, you may provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Age
  • Gender
  • Country or residency
  • Language
  • Interests
  • Bio
  • Profile photo

Account Information

The application may use an account identifier to provide matching, profile and communication features.

Camera and Microphone

Meet Video Call requires camera and microphone access when you use video-call features. Camera and microphone data is used to enable the live communication experience.

Safety and Reports

If you report another user, we may collect information associated with the report, such as the reporting user, reported user, report reason, call identifier and report timestamp.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain your profile
  • Match you with other users
  • Provide random video-call functionality
  • Support language and interest-based matching
  • Provide safety, reporting and blocking features
  • Maintain and improve the application
  • Prevent misuse and enforce community rules
  • Respond to support requests

3. Live Video Calls

When you participate in a video call, your camera and microphone may transmit audio and video to the other participant through the application's communication technology.

You can control camera and microphone permissions through your device settings.

4. Live Messages

Messages exchanged during an active conversation are intended for live communication and are not designed to function as permanent chat history.

5. Matching Information

Information such as your selected interests, language, country and other profile information may be used to improve the matching experience.

6. Blocking and Reporting

Users can block other users and submit reports about inappropriate behavior. Safety-related information may be retained and processed when necessary to investigate reports, enforce community guidelines, prevent abuse, or protect users.

7. Third-Party Services

Meet Video Call uses third-party technology and infrastructure to provide application functionality. These services may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

These services may include Firebase services for authentication and database functionality and WebRTC-related infrastructure for real-time communication.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account through the account deletion option available inside the application.

When an account is deleted, associated profile and account information stored by the application is removed where applicable. Certain safety-related information may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for safety, abuse prevention, legal or other legitimate purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Meet Video Call is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly allow children under 18 to use the service.

If you believe that a person under 18 has provided information through the service, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

11. Your Choices

You can control device permissions such as camera and microphone access through your Android device settings.

You may also use the application's blocking and reporting features and request account deletion through the available account controls.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
